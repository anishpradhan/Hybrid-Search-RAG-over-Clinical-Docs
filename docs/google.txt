1. Overview
Google is a technology company best known for its internet search engine. It is part of Alphabet Inc., which became Google's parent company after a corporate restructuring in 2015. Google's products and services span online search, advertising, maps, video, mobile operating systems, cloud computing, productivity software, and artificial intelligence.

2. Major products and services
Google Search helps people find information on the web. Google Maps provides mapping, navigation, and local discovery features. YouTube is a video-sharing platform where users can watch, upload, and share videos. Android is a mobile operating system used by many device manufacturers. Chrome is a web browser. Gmail is an email service, while Google Drive provides cloud file storage and collaboration.

Google Workspace includes productivity and collaboration tools such as Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, and Drive. Google Cloud offers cloud infrastructure, data analytics, databases, and AI-related services to businesses and developers.

3. Artificial intelligence
Google develops AI models and tools for applications such as language understanding, image processing, coding assistance, search, and productivity. Its AI work is carried out across research and product teams. Developers can access selected AI capabilities through Google Cloud and other developer platforms.

4. Business model
A substantial part of Google's business has historically been associated with advertising. Ads can appear in Search and across other properties and partner networks. Google also earns revenue from subscriptions, cloud services, software, devices, and app-related services.

5. Technology and infrastructure
Google operates large data centers and global network infrastructure to support its services. Reliability, security, privacy, and efficient computing are important concerns because many products serve users around the world.
