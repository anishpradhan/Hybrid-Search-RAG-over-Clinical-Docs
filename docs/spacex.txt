1. Overview
SpaceX, formally Space Exploration Technologies Corp., is an aerospace company founded in 2002 by Elon Musk. The company develops launch vehicles, spacecraft, and satellite-based services. Its stated long-term ambition includes making spaceflight more affordable and enabling human settlement beyond Earth.

2. Launch vehicles
Falcon 9 is a two-stage orbital launch vehicle designed to transport satellites and cargo. Its first stage is capable of returning to Earth and being reused. Reusability can reduce the cost of launching payloads by allowing hardware to fly on multiple missions.

Falcon Heavy is a launch vehicle that uses three Falcon 9 first-stage cores. It is designed to carry heavier payloads than Falcon 9 in suitable mission configurations.

3. Dragon spacecraft
Dragon is a spacecraft used to transport cargo and crew to and from orbit. Crew Dragon is designed to carry astronauts. Cargo Dragon transports supplies and equipment to the International Space Station.

4. Starship
Starship is a fully reusable transportation system under development. It consists of the Starship spacecraft and the Super Heavy booster. The system is intended for missions including transport to Earth orbit, the Moon, and eventually Mars.

5. Starlink
Starlink is a satellite internet service operated by SpaceX. It uses a large constellation of satellites in low Earth orbit to provide broadband connectivity. User terminals communicate with the satellite network, which connects to ground infrastructure and the wider internet.

6. Business model and customers
SpaceX serves commercial satellite operators, government agencies, and other customers that need access to space. Revenue sources include launch services, spacecraft services, and Starlink subscriptions. Launch pricing and mission requirements depend on payload, orbit, and service details.

7. Example retrieval facts
- SpaceX was founded in 2002.
- Falcon 9 is a two-stage orbital launch vehicle.
- Falcon 9's first stage is designed to be reusable.
- Crew Dragon transports astronauts.
- Starlink is a satellite internet service.
- Starship includes the Starship spacecraft and Super Heavy booster.
