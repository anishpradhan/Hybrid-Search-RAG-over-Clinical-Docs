1. Overview
Microsoft is a technology company that develops software, devices, cloud services, and business applications. It was founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen. Its products are used by consumers, developers, schools, and organizations.

2. Major products and services
Windows is Microsoft's operating system for personal computers. Microsoft 365 is a subscription productivity suite that includes applications and services such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and OneDrive. Teams supports chat, meetings, calling, and collaboration.

Azure is Microsoft's cloud computing platform. It provides services for hosting applications, managing databases, networking, analytics, security, and artificial intelligence. GitHub is a platform for hosting and collaborating on software projects. LinkedIn is a professional networking service owned by Microsoft.

3. Artificial intelligence
Microsoft offers AI capabilities through products and cloud services. Azure AI provides tools and infrastructure for building AI applications. Microsoft also integrates AI assistance into selected productivity and developer products. AI systems can support tasks such as summarization, coding, information retrieval, and content generation.

4. Business model
Microsoft earns revenue from software licenses and subscriptions, cloud services, business applications, gaming, devices, and professional networking services. Large organizations often purchase cloud capacity and enterprise software under commercial agreements.

5. Gaming
Xbox is Microsoft's gaming brand. Its activities include gaming consoles, PC games, subscriptions, game development, and online gaming services. Microsoft owns several game studios and publishers.

6. Security and enterprise computing
Microsoft provides identity, endpoint, cloud, and information-protection tools for organizations. Security is important because Windows, Microsoft 365, Azure, and other products are used to manage sensitive business data and critical workloads.
