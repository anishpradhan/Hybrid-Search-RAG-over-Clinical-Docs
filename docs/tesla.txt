1. Overview
Tesla is an electric vehicle and clean-energy company. It designs and manufactures electric vehicles, battery energy storage products, and energy generation and management systems. The company was founded in 2003 by a group of engineers; Elon Musk joined in 2004 as an early investor and later became its chief executive.

2. Electric vehicles
Tesla's vehicle lineup has included Model S, Model 3, Model X, and Model Y, as well as the Cybertruck. Electric vehicles use electric motors powered by battery packs rather than internal combustion engines. Vehicle range depends on factors such as battery capacity, speed, temperature, terrain, and driving behavior.

3. Charging
Tesla operates a Supercharger fast-charging network. Charging speed varies based on the vehicle, charger, battery temperature, state of charge, and other conditions. Home charging is another option for many owners.

4. Energy products
Tesla Energy offers products for energy storage and solar energy. Powerwall is a home battery product designed to store electricity for household use. Megapack is a large-scale battery storage product intended for utilities and commercial energy projects. Solar products convert sunlight into electricity.

5. Software and driver assistance
Tesla vehicles receive software updates and include driver-assistance features. Driver-assistance systems require the driver to remain attentive and responsible for vehicle operation. Such features should not be confused with a guarantee of fully autonomous driving.

6. Manufacturing and supply chain
Electric vehicle manufacturing depends on batteries, electric motors, power electronics, software, and vehicle assembly. Battery production and raw-material supply chains are important factors in cost, production capacity, and scaling.

7. Business model
Tesla earns revenue from vehicle sales, energy generation and storage products, services, charging, and related offerings. Its business is influenced by vehicle demand, production costs, battery prices, competition, and regulation.

8. Example retrieval facts
- Tesla manufactures electric vehicles and energy products.
- Model 3 and Model Y are Tesla vehicle models.
- Powerwall is a home battery product.
- Megapack is designed for large-scale energy storage.
